# Heroes & Generals - Ribbon System (reference notes)

Own-words summary of the ribbon progression in Heroes & Generals (Reto-Moto, 2016-2022 era).
Main data source: a 2016-era community guide (Gamepressure). Numbers may differ in later patches.

## 1. How it works
- A ribbon is a per-activity progression track (do the activity -> earn ribbon XP).
- Each ribbon has 12 levels. Raising a level unlocks either equipment (weapons, vehicles,
  modifications) or a Combat Badge tier (Bronze / Silver / Gold).
- Progress is per soldier (per class/character), not per account.
- Weapon ribbons also hold that weapon's modifications and ammo mod.
- Locked items show the required ribbon in the shop; the loadout screen has a ribbon overview.
- Ribbon Boosters (credits/gold) accelerate ribbon XP.
- A guide states there are 22 ribbons; the unlock list below names more, so the grouping differs (see Uncertain).
- Combat Badges: one slot (two with Veteran status). Tiers B/S/G give stronger effects.
- Ribbon XP is the gate for gear; faction-specific items are shown as US / DE / SU.
